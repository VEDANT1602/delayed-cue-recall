Earlier results on the original seeds, the November 20 extension and the November 21 parameter sweep informed this hypothesis; this registration covers only the following independent seed block.

Primary outcome: mean paired difference in percentage points in 16-step delayed cue choice accuracy after exactly 60 optimizer updates, fixed texture loss minus choice-only. Directional prediction: fixed texture loss lowers mean recall. Use consecutive unexamined training seeds 4000 through 4023 inclusive (24). Compute both modes for each seed. Use the November 21 experiment.py corridor training and evaluation, width 12, Adam at 0.003, texture auxiliary cross-entropy weight 0.5, gradient clipping at 1, 24 matched training pairs per update, 512 matched held-out path pairs per seed. No other settings or new endpoints enter this test. Include all seeds with ties; no interim reporting or seed replacement. Give both means, the paired difference, and a two-sided 95% percentile interval on mean difference from 20,000 resamples of seed pairs using NumPy PCG64 seed 42117. Treat the directional result as supported for this generator if the upper endpoint is below zero; otherwise report it as inconclusive, or contradictory if the interval is entirely positive. Runtime failures may be repaired and all affected pairs rerun without changing the design, with any correction recorded.
