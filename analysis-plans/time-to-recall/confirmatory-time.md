# Confirmatory GRU time-to-recall experiment
21 November 2026. The fixed-update vanilla-RNN results were known when this plan was written. Other architecture pilot jobs were launched before this record but their outcomes had not been inspected when this directional prediction was specified. Only the 16 GRU seed pairs below are reserved for this publicly timestamped test. No result from the reserved block may be inspected before public posting.

Question: in the cue-balanced four-texture corridor, does adding fixed next-texture weight 0.5 delay acquisition of terminal-cue recall by a width-12 GRU trained with Adam at 0.003? Use exactly the code and defaults in timecourse.py, 24 training pairs per update, training delays 2, 4 and 8, gradient-norm clipping at 1, stop after 200 updates. Run training seeds 7900–7915 inclusive in cue-only and auxiliary conditions, paired by initialization and data. Use the same distinct evaluation panel of 256 cue-balanced pairs at delay 16 for both arms within seed. Evaluate every 10 updates. An event is recorded at the SECOND of two consecutive scheduled visits scoring at least 90% correct. Otherwise right-censor at 200. Apply this rule to all 16 pairs, with no seed replacement or interim selection. With administrative censoring only, the restricted mean acquisition time at 200 is the arithmetic mean of event times truncated at 200. An event at 200 is counted as an event.

Primary endpoint: paired mean difference, auxiliary minus cue-only, in restricted acquisition time at delay 16. Positive means slower acquisition with the auxiliary head. Draw 20,000 percentile bootstrap resamples of seed pairs using NumPy PCG64 seed 19373, two-sided 95%. A positive lower limit supports the prediction; an interval crossing or touching zero is inconclusive; a negative upper limit supports the opposite direction. Report both arm event counts and mean restricted times even if the result is inconclusive. Other thresholds, delays, architectures and measured tasks are sensitivity or exploratory results. This plan is a prospective follow-on to the earlier vanilla experiment, not a registration of any earlier data or of pilots. Record the actual public commit and UTC execution times.
